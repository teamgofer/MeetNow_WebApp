# MeetNow Mobility Fleet - User Guide

This guide provides instructions for using the MeetNow Mobility Fleet System for both fleet owners/operators and riders.

## For Fleet Owners and Operators

### Getting Started as a Fleet Owner

1. **Register Your Fleet Company**
   - Navigate to the Mobility section in your MeetNow dashboard
   - Click "Register Fleet Company"
   - Complete the company profile:
     - Company name
     - Contact information
     - Service area (draw on map)
     - Upload company logo
   - Submit for verification

2. **Add Vehicles to Your Fleet**
   - From your Fleet Dashboard, select "Add Vehicle"
   - Choose the vehicle type (Paddle Cab, Golf Cart, etc.)
   - Complete vehicle details:
     - Vehicle name/identifier
     - Maximum passengers
     - Upload photos
     - Set hourly rate (in credits)
   - Activate the vehicle when ready for service

3. **Manage Your Fleet Staff**
   - Invite operators through the "Staff Management" section
   - Assign roles (operator, manager, maintenance)
   - Set permissions and access levels
   - Monitor staff performance metrics

### Operating Your Fleet

1. **Dashboard Overview**
   - Real-time map of all your vehicles
   - Current status of each vehicle
   - Active ride information
   - Pending ride requests
   - Daily/weekly/monthly earnings

2. **Vehicle Management**
   - View detailed status of each vehicle
   - Schedule maintenance
   - Assign vehicles to specific operators
   - Track vehicle utilization and earnings

3. **Managing Ride Requests**
   - View incoming ride requests
   - Accept/decline requests based on availability
   - See pickup and destination locations
   - View estimated credits and ride details

4. **Setting Pricing Strategy**
   - Configure base rates for each vehicle type
   - Set up dynamic pricing for peak times
   - Create special rates for meetup events
   - Offer promotional packages

### For Vehicle Operators

1. **Getting Started as an Operator**
   - Accept invitation from fleet owner
   - Complete operator profile
   - Download the MeetNow operator interface
   - Complete safety training (if required)

2. **Daily Operations**
   - Start shift by marking yourself available
   - Accept incoming ride requests
   - Navigate to pickup locations
   - Complete rides and collect ratings
   - Track your earnings in real-time

3. **Using the Operator Interface**
   - View your current status
   - See ride details and navigation
   - Communicate with riders
   - Report issues with the vehicle
   - Emergency assistance access

4. **Best Practices**
   - Keep your availability status updated
   - Follow recommended routes
   - Provide excellent customer service
   - Report maintenance issues promptly
   - Position yourself in high-demand areas

## For Riders

### Finding and Requesting Rides

1. **Discover Nearby Vehicles**
   - Open the MeetNow app
   - Tap the "Mobility" icon on the map
   - See available vehicles near your location
   - Different icons represent different vehicle types

2. **Requesting a Ride**
   - Tap on a vehicle or use the "Request Ride" button
   - Enter your destination
   - Select number of passengers
   - View estimated credits
   - Choose vehicle type preference (optional)
   - Tap "Request Ride"

3. **One-Click Meetup Transport**
   - When viewing a meetup, tap "Get a Ride"
   - The app automatically sets the meetup location as destination
   - Follow the standard request process
   - Meetup attendees may see special group rates

### During Your Ride

1. **Preparing for Pickup**
   - Once a ride is accepted, you'll see operator information
   - Track the vehicle's approach on the map
   - Receive ETA updates
   - Use the chat feature to communicate with your operator

2. **In-Ride Experience**
   - View ride progress on the map
   - See estimated arrival time
   - Use emergency assistance if needed
   - Change destination (additional credits may apply)

3. **Completing Your Ride**
   - Credits are automatically deducted at ride completion
   - Rate your operator (1-5 stars)
   - Leave optional feedback
   - Report any issues or lost items

### Managing Your Rides

1. **Ride History**
   - View all your past rides
   - See details including:
     - Route taken
     - Credits spent
     - Operator rating
     - Date and time

2. **Favorite Operators and Fleets**
   - Save operators or fleet companies as favorites
   - Request rides preferentially from your favorites
   - Create saved routes for frequent trips

3. **Group Rides**
   - Coordinate rides for multiple people
   - Split credit costs among participants
   - Schedule pickups for meetup attendees

## Credits and Payments

1. **Understanding Pricing**
   - Base rate varies by vehicle type
   - Distance affects total cost
   - Number of passengers affects pricing
   - Peak times may have surge pricing
   - Special rates may apply for meetup events

2. **Managing Credits**
   - The same credit system used for meetups applies
   - View your credit balance in your profile
   - Purchase additional credits as needed
   - View credit transaction history

3. **Tipping Operators**
   - Optional tips can be added after ride completion
   - Choose from suggested amounts or enter custom amount
   - Tips are paid in credits through the app

## Safety and Support

1. **Safety Features**
   - Real-time tracking of all rides
   - Verified operators and vehicles
   - In-app emergency assistance
   - Share ride details with friends or family

2. **Reporting Issues**
   - Report vehicle or operator issues
   - Report lost items
   - Request ride refunds if warranted
   - Rate and review your experience

3. **Getting Support**
   - In-app chat support
   - Email support at mobility@meetnow.app
   - Emergency support hotline: [Number]
   - View FAQs and help articles

## Best Practices

1. **For Fleet Owners**
   - Verify all operators thoroughly
   - Maintain vehicles in excellent condition
   - Respond promptly to maintenance reports
   - Analyze performance data to optimize operations

2. **For Operators**
   - Maintain professional appearance and conduct
   - Keep your vehicle clean and comfortable
   - Communicate clearly with riders
   - Follow all local regulations

3. **For Riders**
   - Be ready at your pickup location
   - Treat vehicles and operators with respect
   - Report any safety concerns immediately
   - Provide constructive feedback
